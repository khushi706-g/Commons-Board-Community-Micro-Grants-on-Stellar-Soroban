# Demo video script (1–2 minutes)

Use two browser profiles (or two devices) to represent a bounty poster and a contributor.

1. **(0:00–0:15) Hook** — "This is a community bounty board. Post a job with
   money attached, someone does it, they get paid, and it becomes part of
   their public on-chain track record."

2. **(0:15–0:35) Post a bounty** — As the poster, pin a bounty with a
   reward. Show the deposit transaction confirm and the card land on the
   board with a green pin.

3. **(0:35–0:55) Submit & approve** — Switch to the contributor, submit
   work with a note. Show the pin flip to yellow (in review) and
   `SubmissionMade` land in the live feed. Switch back to the poster,
   approve the submission — show the payout transaction and the pin flip to
   blue (paid).

4. **(0:55–1:15) Contributor record** — Switch to the Contributors view,
   look up the contributor's address, show their score and completed count
   go up — this is the cross-contract call landing.

5. **(1:15–1:30) Wrap-up** — Resize to phone width to show mobile
   responsiveness, show `cargo test --workspace` passing, and show the
   GitHub Actions CI run green.

Keep narration plain and specific — name what's on screen, not what it's
"powered by."
